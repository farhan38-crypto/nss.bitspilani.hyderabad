About the scheme

Service has a history. We make it local.

NSS was launched by the Government of India on 24 September 1969, on the birth centenary of Mahatma Gandhi.

The idea

“Not me, but you.”

The NSS emblem uses the Konark wheel as a symbol of the cycle of creation and continuity. Its red and blue colours represent the energy of young volunteers and the larger cosmos in which service takes place.

At BITS Pilani Hyderabad, that philosophy becomes practical: students work together on initiatives that respond to community, health, education and environmental needs.

Our unit

What NSS looks like here.

People first

We work with students, school communities, healthcare professionals, campus staff and other community members.

Hands-on service

Our activities are built around participation: collecting waste, donating blood, interacting with children, and recognising everyday contributors.

Campus responsibility

The unit connects national service ideals to the realities and communities around BITS Pilani Hyderabad Campus.

24 September 1969 — NSS launched nationally.

The recent work shown on this website reflects the same broad spirit: community health through a blood donation and eye check-up camp, environmental responsibility through cleanliness work, and social connection through Independence Day activities with students at MPPS Pothaipally.
