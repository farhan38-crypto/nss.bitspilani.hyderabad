Who we are

A campus community that believes service is a habit.

NSS BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus brings students together for community-oriented work across health, education, environment, rural development and social awareness.

From a blood donation and eye check-up camp to cleanliness drives and spending Independence Day with school students, the unit's recent work is rooted in showing up where help and participation matter.

Our canvas

Six ways we turn volunteering into action.

Different projects, one principle: use student energy for something larger than ourselves.

Health & blood

Blood donation and eye check-up initiatives connecting students, healthcare professionals and donors.

Environment

Cleanliness drives and sustainability-focused work that makes responsibility visible on campus.

Education

Learning and interaction with school communities, including Independence Day activities at MPPS Pothaipally.

Community care

Small acts of appreciation for the people who keep the campus community moving every day.

Rural outreach

Community-facing initiatives that take service beyond the campus boundary.

Sustainability

Celebrating collective campus efforts towards environmental awareness and a greener future.
